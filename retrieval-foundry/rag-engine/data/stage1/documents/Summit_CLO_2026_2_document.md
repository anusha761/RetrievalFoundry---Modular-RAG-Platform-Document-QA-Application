## 3. Interest and Principal Terms

If the reference rate becomes unavailable, the transaction documents provide for a designated replacement benchmark and related fallback mechanics.

Interest is generally payable on each scheduled payment date. The reinvestment period runs from June 30, 2026 through June 30, 2029, subject to the transaction's stated termination conventions. During this period, the manager may reinvest principal proceeds in eligible collateral and may sell assets for permitted reasons.
