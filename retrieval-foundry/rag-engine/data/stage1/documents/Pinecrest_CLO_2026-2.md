## 5. Investment Risk Considerations

Investors should consider the following risks: (1) Credit Risk: The risk that borrowers of the underlying loans will fail to make payments. (3) Reinvestment Risk: The risk that the Manager will be unable to find replacement assets with sufficient yield during the 5-year reinvestment window.
